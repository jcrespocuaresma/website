Jesús Crespo Cuaresma

Professor of Economics, Vienna University of Economics and Business. Research on macroeconomics, economic growth, forecasting, demography, climate change, migration and development.

Home Research Teaching

I am Professor of Economics at the Department of Economics of Vienna University of Economics and Business. My work combines applied econometrics, macroeconomic modelling and quantitative approaches to long-run economic, demographic and environmental change.

I am affiliated with the Wittgenstein Centre for Demography and Global Human Capital and work as scientific consultant at the Austrian Institute of Economic Research. I have held academic and research positions at the University of Innsbruck, the University of Vienna, the International Institute for Applied Systems Analysis, Vilnius University and the Norwegian School of Economics.

Research areas

Macroeconomics and forecasting

DSGE models, forecast combinations, fiscal multipliers, monetary economics and international macroeconomics.

Growth and development

Human capital, convergence, poverty, demographic change and long-run income projections.

Climate and population

Greenhouse gas emissions, climate vulnerability, migration, food security and socioeconomic scenarios.
