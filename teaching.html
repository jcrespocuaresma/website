Teaching

I teach undergraduate, master and doctoral courses in macroeconomics, econometrics and economic growth, with an emphasis on analytical clarity, empirical tools and the connection between theory and policy.

Courses and topics

  • Introductory and advanced macroeconomics
  • Macroeconomic policy and international macroeconomics
  • Econometrics and time series analysis
  • Bayesian econometrics, macroeconomics and economic growth at doctoral level
  • Specialized courses on economic growth and macroeconometrics for international organizations and policy institutions

Supervision

I supervise master and doctoral theses in fiscal policy, economic growth, development economics, monetary economics, international economics, environmental economics, econometrics and labour economics.

Theses supervised in these areas have received national and international awards, including prizes awarded by the Oesterreichische Nationalbank.

Teaching recognition

  • Best Teacher Award, Executive MBA Bucharest Program, 2021–2023.
  • WU Award for Excellence in Teaching, 2022 and 2016.
  • WU Award for Innovative Teaching, 2017.
  • Lecturer of the Year, Faculty of Economics and Statistics, University of Innsbruck, 2008.
