Jesús Crespo Cuaresma

Professor of Economics · Vienna University of Economics and Business
Head of the Department of Economics

I am an economist working on econometric methods and their application to questions in macroeconomics, demographic change, climate change and human mobility. A central theme of my research is the development of quantitative tools for studying long-run socioeconomic change under model uncertainty.
