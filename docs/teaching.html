Teaching

My teaching covers macroeconomics, econometrics and economic growth at undergraduate, master's and doctoral level.

Areas

  • Introductory and advanced macroeconomics
  • Macroeconomic policy and international macroeconomics
  • Econometrics and time-series analysis
  • Bayesian econometrics
  • Economic growth and development

Doctoral and specialised teaching

At doctoral level, I teach courses in econometrics, macroeconomics and economic growth. I have also taught specialised courses in economic growth and macroeconometrics for World Bank staff and as part of international capacity-building programmes.

Thesis supervision

I supervise master's and doctoral research in fiscal and monetary policy, economic growth and development, international economics, environmental economics, labour economics and econometrics.
