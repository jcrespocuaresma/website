Research

My research combines methodological work in econometrics with substantive questions in macroeconomics, demography and environmental change. Across these areas, I am particularly interested in settings in which uncertainty is substantial and relationships evolve over time.

Econometric methods and forecasting

A major strand of my work develops econometric methods for model uncertainty, structural change and complex interdependencies. This includes Bayesian model averaging, spatial econometrics, global vector autoregressive models, forecast combination and methods for detecting structural changes in complex econometric systems.

  • Bayesian Indicator-Saturated Regression for Climate Policy Evaluation, The Econometrics Journal, forthcoming.
  • Forecasting with Global Vector Autoregressive Models: A Bayesian Approach, Journal of Applied Econometrics, 2016.
  • Spatial Filtering, Model Uncertainty and the Speed of Income Convergence in Europe, Journal of Applied Econometrics, 2013.

Demography and long-run development

A second strand studies how population change, educational attainment and human capital shape long-run economic development. This work has contributed to socioeconomic projection frameworks used in research on future development and climate-change risks.

  • Education Rather than Age Structure Brings Demographic Dividend, PNAS, 2019.
  • Income Projections for Climate Change Research: A Framework Based on Human Capital Dynamics, Global Environmental Change, 2017.
  • Is the Demographic Dividend an Education Dividend?, Demography, 2014.
  • The Demography of Educational Attainment and Economic Growth, Science, 2008.

Climate, environment and human mobility

More recent work focuses on interactions between environmental shocks and socioeconomic outcomes, including greenhouse-gas emissions, climate vulnerability, conflict and migration. A recurring objective is to identify the mechanisms and local conditions that shape heterogeneous responses to environmental change.

  • A Unified Modelling Framework for Projecting Sectoral Greenhouse Gas Emissions, Communications Earth & Environment, 2024.
  • Natural Resources Modulate the Nexus Between Environmental Shocks and Human Mobility, Nature Communications, 2023.
  • A Meta-Analysis of Country-Level Studies on Environmental Change and Migration, Nature Climate Change, 2020.
  • Climate, Conflict and Forced Migration, Global Environmental Change, 2019.
