Research

My research uses quantitative methods to study macroeconomic dynamics, long-run development, demographic change, climate-related risks and the interaction between economic systems and environmental change.

Main fields

  • Macroeconomic forecasting and policy. Forecast combinations, DSGE models, fiscal multipliers, monetary economics and business cycle synchronization.
  • Economic growth and development. Human capital, income convergence, poverty, regional growth and long-run projections.
  • Climate, demography and migration. Climate change vulnerability, greenhouse gas emissions, food security, internal and international migration, and socioeconomic pathways.
  • Applied econometrics. Model uncertainty, Bayesian methods, structural breaks, spatial models and predictive evaluation.
